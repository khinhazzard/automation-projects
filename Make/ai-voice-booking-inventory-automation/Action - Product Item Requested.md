Extract and save the exact SKU copied directly from the matching product entry in the dedicated product catalog table in the configured Knowledge Base for the product checked by the `check_inventory` action. Store only the SKU, not the product name or description.

Examples:
- Gas Control Valve → WH-045
- Expansion Tank → WH-005
- PEX Crimp Fitting Assortment → PL-055

Never guess, infer, construct, or substitute a SKU. If the exact product and its SKU cannot be verified, do not save a guessed SKU or substitute the product name. Only update this field with the verified SKU of the product actually checked.
